# Vermont road closures, July 2023 flood

63 closed state-highway segments (lines) during the July 10-11, 2023 Vermont flood.
- Source: VTrans Traffic Management Center notifications, digitized by S. Grant (2025), "RoadClosures2023", FEMC / UVM. License CC0.
- Snapshot times: closures listed on 2023-07-11 23:40 and 2023-07-12 09:45 (no per-row time).
- Fields: Road, Cause (51 "Flooding", plus high water, washout, sinkhole, mudslide), FullorPart (58 full, 5 partial).
- Use: second, independent test storm for the closure model (validation only, not training).
