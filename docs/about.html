About

Mass Flood Watch

PhD research at Worcester Polytechnic Institute. It shows which roads in central Massachusetts a flash flood is likely to close, and who would lose quick access to a hospital, fire station or police station.

The idea

Narrow enough for a road crew

Most flood warnings describe an area: a county, a river, a zone on a map. Road crews and emergency managers need something narrower: which stretches of road will have water on them, when, and what that does to the rest of the network.

Road by road

Every road segment gets its own chance of closing.

Ahead of time

Runs on the two-day rain forecast when a storm is coming.

The knock-on effect

Who is cut off from help when roads close.

Open

Public data and open code, so anyone can check it.

Terms of use

A research prototype

Mass Flood Watch is under active development. It is not an official warning service and is not affiliated with any state or federal agency. Do not use it to decide whether a road is safe to drive.

In an emergency

Call 911. For official flood watches and warnings, see the National Weather Service, Boston / Norton.

Mass Flood Watch

Which roads will a flash flood close, and who loses quick access to a hospital, fire station or police? A research project at Worcester Polytechnic Institute that turns radar rain into a road-by-road flood picture for central Massachusetts.

PhD research by Mohab Eldokla. Advisors: Prof. Mingjiang Tao, Prof. Leonard Albano and Prof. Lindsay Graff.
