Model tree (printable)

The content of the interactive model tree as one page. Parameter values are read from the code (proto/model_tree.py, check: CHECK.md).

1. Inputs

Observed rain (radar + gauges) live

Radar plus rain gauges tell us how much rain fell on every 1 km square, every hour.

NOAA MRMS MultiSensor QPE 01H Pass2: radar precipitation bias-corrected with rain gauges. Before 14 Oct 2020 the gauge-corrected GaugeCorr QPE (IEM archive); RadarOnly only as a last resort, recorded per hour.

mm per hour · 0.01° (~1 km), hourly · Oct 2020 → today (GaugeCorr before)

Out: rain r(x, y, t), mm per hour on 20 m cells

Goes to: Rain on every cell (observed rain, mm/h (1 km)); Radar spin-up + nowcast (12 h observed rain)

Cannot capture: Radar under-reads in the heaviest convective cores and far from the radar; gauges are sparse.

Sources: NOAA NSSL / NCEP, MRMS MultiSensor QPE 01H Pass2 (AWS noaa-mrms-pds); Zhang J. et al. (2016) Multi-Radar Multi-Sensor (MRMS) quantitative precipitation estimation. BAMS 97(4)

Code: sim/colab/w9_lisflood.py mrms_sources / mrms_sample

Forecast rain + 2 m temperature (HRRR) live

A weather-forecast model says how much rain will fall in the next two days, every hour, on 3 km squares.

NOAA HRRR v4 hourly precipitation buckets (APCP) and 2 m air temperature; 48 h cycles at 00/06/12/18 UTC, 18 h cycles every hour.

mm per hour; °C · 3 km, hourly · live, each cycle

Out: forecast rain and temperature fields per forecast hour

Goes to: Spreading the forecast (20 km / 90th percentile) (forecast rain, mm/h (3 km)); Rain or snow (2 m temperature, °C); Rain gate (town 6 h / 24 h maxima)

Cannot capture: A 3 km forecast cannot place a thunderstorm to the street; storms are often displaced by tens of km.

Sources: NOAA GSL / NCEP, High-Resolution Rapid Refresh (HRRR) v4 (AWS noaa-hrrr-bdp-pds); Dowell D.C. et al. (2022) The High-Resolution Rapid Refresh (HRRR): an hourly updating convection-allowing forecast model. Part I. Weather and Forecasting 37(8)

Code: sim/colab/hrrr_rain.py hourly / nbhd; sim/colab/lf_phase.py tmp2m
