# Forward-test change 1 (written 5 Nov 2026, before it is applied)

Pre-registered under `PROSPECTIVE.md` ("Changes during the window"). Approved by the advisors on 5 Nov 2026. It takes effect with the first
live cycle built from the commit that adds this file. Ledger rows before it carry `settings` without `terrain=T2 start=rain` (model **v1**),
rows after it carry them (model **v2**). Results are reported for each version separately, and storms that straddle the change are scored
with the cycle that actually issued each forecast.

## Why

The 5 Nov 2026 06Z cycle listed 22 likely closures. Most of them were start-state water, not storm water:

- Downtown Worcester (MLK Jr Blvd 45256/45260, Foster St 45277/45281, 12771, Major Taylor Blvd 45280/45283, Bridge St 38477): the known
  terrain artefacts (`docs/proto/terrain/TERRAIN.md`). MLK Jr Blvd passed 30 cm 0.8 h into the run and Foster St at 7 h, while the HRRR
  rain over the box was 0 mm for the first 11 h.
- Box b2 (Berlin / Northborough / Clinton): `closed_per_frame` was 5–7 in the first hour and 0 from hour 5.5 on, through the storm. The peak
  depths came from the start-up transient (channels start a third full and spill into low ground), not from the rain.

## What changes (both, together)

1. **Terrain fix T2** in the live flood runs: `LF_NOTCH_FIX=1 LF_CHAN_ENFORCE=1` (`forecast.yml`, `run_forecast.slurm`, `forecast_d3.slurm`).
   Tested on all 25 storms (`TERRAIN.md`): site recall Δ 0.000 (lower bound −0.053), FSS −0.0001, alert-tier lift 6.3 → 7.2, USGS gauge
   error slightly lower, segments with peak > 2 m 12 → 1. Adopted on a non-inferiority reading (Δ lower bound > −0.06, FSS within −0.001,
   gauges not worse) with the advisors' approval. The displayed replays already use T2 (owner decision, 1 Nov).
2. **Depth above the rain-onset state** in the closure rule: `PROTO_RES_CONSISTENT=1 PROTO_RC_START_H=rain` (`proto/prototype.py`). The
   start-up state is the depth snapshot at the first hour in which the box-mean rain since the start of the run reaches 1.0 mm
   (`RC_RAIN_ONSET_MM`, fixed here, not tuned; at least 0.5 h, the existing `RC_START_H`). Closure depth = min(hmax, snapshot peak + 0.10 m)
   less that start-up depth; the closure rule itself (P = Φ((depth − 0.30) / 0.10), likely = ≥ 2 of 8) is unchanged. In the status and the
   ensemble (`forecast.py run_summary`, `forecast_kofn.py closures`) a segment already at ≥ 30 cm in the onset frame is counted as
   "standing water at start", never as a closure.

Nothing else changes: gate, HRRR set-up, neighbourhood, CN × 0.88, `LF_PARITY=1`, infiltration, 20 m, the 2-of-8 rule, the scoring.

## Known effect

Fewer flagged segments (on the 25 hindcast storms T2 alone took 779 → 586 flags at unchanged recall). A road that already holds water when
the rain begins needs 30 cm **more** to count, which can under-count a real closure at a spot that is wet before the storm. This is
recorded as a limit of model v2.

## Operational fix, 6 Nov 2026 (not a model change)

The gate placed the same 20 km box up to three times when the heaviest rain fell in District 3 towns east of the modelled area
(`D3_BOX` ends at x = 206 km; Natick, Wayland, Sherborn, Framingham lie beyond): the box was clamped back inside, did not cover the peak,
the peak was never retired, and the next box landed on it again. The 12Z 5 Nov and 00Z 6 Nov cycles ran b2 = b3 = b4, so 16 of 32
flood jobs repeated b2 and the northern towns over the threshold (Fitchburg, Westminster, Gardner, Ashby, Hubbardston) got no box. Now the
peak is always retired and a repeated box is skipped. Cycles built after this commit (from 6 Nov 06Z) carry the fix; earlier rows are scored as they ran.
