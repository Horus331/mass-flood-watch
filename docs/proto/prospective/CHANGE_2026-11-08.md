# Change to the live forecast, 8 Nov 2026: P1 rise rule

**Adopted** 8 Nov 2026 (Mo, after review with the advisors). Proposed and pre-registered in `CHANGE_PROPOSAL_2026-11-07.md` (P1) before
it was tested; the test results are in that file, `p1_rise_test.json` (parity catalogue) and `p1_rise_test_t2.json` (T2 runs).

**Rule.** A forecast run counts a road segment as closed when P(close) >= 0.5, it is not already at >= 30 cm at rain onset, and its depth
rises at least 30 cm above its rain-onset depth at some frame after onset (`proto/forecast.py` `rises`, `RISE_CM = 30`). Used by
`forecast.run_summary` (single-run lists) and `forecast_kofn.closures` (the k-of-8 ensemble count). Segments dropped by the rise rule are
counted in `under_30cm_rise` of the run summary.

**Evidence.** No reported flood site lost in any pool; flagged segments down 37 to 42 % (parity catalogue, out of sample) and 19 to 49 %
(T2 runs). Ledger settings from the first cycle after this change: `... terrain=T2 start=rain rise=30`.

**Not changed.** The storm replays, the storm archive's radar reruns (frozen BASELINE.md settings) and the scoring of past storms.
