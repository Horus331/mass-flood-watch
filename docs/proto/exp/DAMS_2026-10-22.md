|---|---|---|---|---|

The dam runs flag more roads. In Leominster that buys a few more hits, in Worcester none, so lift falls in both. With dams the Fitchburg and Leominster gauge rises flip from slight over- to clear under-prediction (0.51 vs 1.00 m, 2.51 vs 2.92 m).
Verdict: keep dams off by default. Both settings changed at once (dams and cfl 0.3), so this does not isolate the dam effect. The runs are published in docs/proto/turing/*_dams/.
