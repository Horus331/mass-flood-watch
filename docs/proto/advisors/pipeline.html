Road-closure prototype: from rain to closed roads

Mass Flood Watch · tested on the 11 Sept 2023 Leominster flash flood (MassDOT District 3) · state of the code on 22 Oct 2026

1 · Inputs

What the storm falls on
RainNOAA MRMS radar + gauge QPE, 1 km, hourly
TerrainUSGS 3DEP lidar DEM, 10 m
SoilsUSDA SSURGO soil group → NRCS curve number
Land coverNLCD 2021 impervious → runoff, roughness
ChannelsNHDPlus HR streams, bankfull size burnt in
Crossings29k road–stream crossings, NAACC culvert sizes; opened through road fill

2 · Flood model

LISFLOOD-FP 8.2
2-D shallow waterLocal-inertial solver (Bates et al. 2010), open source, Univ. of Bristol
Grid10–20 m cells, open boundaries, 36–48 h of storm
Rain lossesNRCS curve number (TR-55, AMC II), no tuning
Runs on GPU (WPI Turing) or CPU (GitHub Actions)

3 · Hazard

Water on every road
Depthpeak depth, hours above 30 cm, every 30 min
Timingwhen water arrives and peaks
Speed opt-inpeak velocity, depth × velocity
Flow at crossingsdischarge through each culvert notch

4 · Closure rules

Will this segment close?
Depth rule defaultP = Φ((depth − 0.30 m) / 0.10 m)30 cm stops an ordinary car
Culvert rule opt-inFHWA HDS-5 capacity vs modelled flow, with surveyed blockage
Overtop candidate map layerstream reaches the road crest: shown as “may close”, not counted in P
