# ML / cascade review — 18 Oct 2026 (internal reviewer)

## Facts that drive the plan
1. **Labels for the 4 engine storms are near-zero**: storm-day road-flood reports inside flood_reports.json: Dec 2023 = 9, Sep 2023 = 19, Jul 2023 = 11, Jan 2018 = 0 → 39 positives total; 2,136 of 4,551 reports are crash records coded "water on road". No probability model can be calibrated on 39 positives.
2. **HWMs cover only 2 of the 4 storms** (Jul 2023: 105 riverine, western box; Jan 2018: 85 coastal). Dec 2023 and Sep 2023 have none.
3. **Engine outputs are not ML-ready**: frames are 6-class uint8 PNGs; velocity is computed but never written; roads get max depth only (no velocity, no duration).
4. **Graph attributes partly imputed**: 25% of AADT exactly 1,000 and 8% exactly 1,500 (defaults); 26% without road name; cls only 0/1/2.
5. **The physics engine currently loses to the statistical ranker for warning** (findings.json: forecast-driven water map caught 2–4% of flooded segments for Sep 2023; GBM/GNN ranker AUC 0.93–0.94). Position the surrogate as a fast emulator for cascade/what-if and as a physics feature for the ranker, not as a replacement.
6. **Compute**: statewide 30 m ≈ 1.5–2 h per run on an L4 → corpus of ~25–40 statewide runs in 6 weeks; use district/HUC8 boxes (≈ 4 M cells, 10× cheaper) for most of the corpus.

## Plan (≈ 44 working days)
1. **Emit surrogate targets** (2 d): per cell float16 `hmax`, `t_first`, `dur`, `vmax`, `hv_max`; per segment mean and p90 depth and velocity (not just max); 3-h float16 depth on a 90 m grid; `run_manifest.json`.
2. **Training corpus** (3 d + 1 week wall clock): 20 highest-report days 2015–2026 (MRMS; AORC for 2002–2014), district boxes, parameter ensemble FC_SCALE {0.5,1,2} × WET_START {0,60,120} × n {0.035,0.06,0.10} × rain {0.8,1,1.2}; queue file on HF pulled by Colab/Kaggle launchers.
3. **Segment-level spatio-temporal GNN surrogate** (5 d): targets first-wet frame / peak depth / hours ≥ 30 cm; inputs H-V-C statics + hourly rain + **upstream-accumulated rain** routed down drain10 + antecedent + physics scalars; GraphSAGE/GAT + GRU (PyG Temporal) with downhill-water edges; Huber on log1p depth + BCE + rain-monotonicity penalty. ≤ 1 s statewide inference.
4. **Tile U-Net grid surrogate** (6 d, stretch): 128→64 tiles, FiLM on scalars, volume-consistency and HAND constraints; cite Löwe 2021, Guo 2021, Bentivoglio 2023, Fraehr 2023. Call it "physics-emulating", not PINN.
5. **Expand observations** (4 d): USGS gauge peak stage + timing for all 4 storms (~120 MA stage gauges); engine runs on the 20 report-days → hundreds of storm-day positives; NWS Storm Events; Sentinel-1 for Jul/Dec 2023 (stretch). Fix the HWM comparison to water-surface elevation.
6. **Evaluation protocol** written before training (4 d): leave-one-storm-out + spatial block CV by HUC10; CSI/F1 at 10/30/50 cm, arrival-time MAE, PR-AUC; baselines HAND (Atlas 14), FEMA SFHA, existing GBM/GNN, lowest-roads-first; reliability diagrams + Brier skill with bootstrap CIs; uncertainty decomposed (physics ensemble, HRRR members, deep ensemble); pre-register thresholds in docs/validation/protocol.md.
7. **Replace the cascade heuristic** (5 d). Weaknesses now: "cut off" = no path to a cls≥1 route (arbitrary); detour 60/40 to neighbours is not routing, over-counts along corridors, applies 24-h AADT to a 30-min frame, ignores capacity, 33% imputed AADT. Proposed: link passability from depth/velocity (Pregnolato 2017 speed–depth curve, h·v ≤ 0.3–0.5), accessibility loss to hospitals/fire/EMS (MassGIS layers) via Dijkstra travel time, detour by link-removal rerouting with v/c from class capacities + MassDOT Road Inventory lanes, hour-of-day AADT factors × duration → vehicle-hours; optional LEHD LODES OD; validate on MassDOT continuous count stations on storm days; flag imputed AADT.
8. **Culvert/bridge inventory + hydraulic exceedance** (5 d): NBI (items 43, 61, 71, 113), MassDOT culvert inventory (GeoDOT), NAACC crossings; per crossing modelled discharge vs capacity (HDS-5), HW/D, overtopping; probability = share of the ensemble exceeding; validate against repeating flood points and NBI item 71.
9. **Failure-probability hierarchy** (3 d): L1 road impassable (validated, calibrated) → L2 hydraulic exceedance (computed, ensemble uncertainty, indirectly validated) → L3 structural failure (literature fragility, expert-based). Tag every output validated / computed / assumed. OpenFEMA PA projects and NFIP claims as coarse damage checks.

11. **Paper 2**: from water depth to network consequence under uncertainty (TR-D / RESS / IJDRR).
