Mass Flood Watch

Which roads will a flash flood close, and who loses quick access to a hospital, fire station or police? A research project at Worcester Polytechnic Institute that turns radar rain into a road-by-road flood picture for central Massachusetts.

PhD research by Mohab Eldokla. Advisors: Prof. Mingjiang Tao, Prof. Leonard Albano and Prof. Lindsay Graff.
