# Free / public data we are not yet using (or only partly using)

Pass of 19 Oct 2026. For each source: URL, coverage, format, access, licence, and **exactly where it plugs in**:
`INPUT` (engine forcing or static layer), `CAL` (calibration target), `VAL` (held-out validation under `docs/validation/protocol.md`), `LABEL` (ML target/positive),
`BASE` (baseline the protocol compares against). **BIAS** marks sources that directly test the ~1–3 m high water levels on steep western-MA reaches
(South River/Conway, Avery Brook, West Brook — July 2023) or the over-wet roads of Dec 2023.

Already used (for reference): 3DEP 10 m DEM, MRMS 1-h and 24-h Pass-2 QPE, NLCD 2021 impervious, SSURGO (hydgrp, wtd, aws0100wta, brockdepmin), NHDPlus HR
flowlines, USGS IV discharge at boundary gauges (+ some in-box stage/flow in `data/gauges/`), USGS STN HWMs (`stn_ma.json`), MassDOT road-flood reports, NOAA CO-OPS
tides, MA Stream Crossings (peak flows, bankfull, deck), NBI 2024, WBD HU10, HRRR (forecast mode).

Access rules for this project: no sign-ups, emails or tokens by the agent. Items marked **needs Mo** require a free account or key that only Mo can create.

## 0. Urgent infrastructure note — USGS legacy web services are being switched off
`sim/gauges_fetch.py` calls `https://waterservices.usgs.gov/nwis/iv/`. USGS decommission plan: Campaign 3 (**Nov 2026 – Feb 2027**) removes legacy `/uv`, `/peaks`,
`/ratings`, revisions and **all WaterServices URLs**; WaterServices fully decommissioned **Q1 2027**; intentional degradation/blackouts possible from Aug 2026
([USGS blog](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/blog/nwisweb-decommission-summary/), [campaign 2](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/blog/nwisweb-decommission-campaign2/)).
Replacement: OGC **Water Data APIs** at `https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/ogcapi/v0/` (collections: continuous, daily, field-measurements, channel-measurements,
monitoring-locations, time-series metadata). Works without a key at low rate limits; an API key raises limits (**needs Mo** if we hit limits)
([API root](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/ogcapi/v0/)). Peaks and ratings replacements are not listed yet — download what we need from the legacy endpoints **before
Nov 2026**. Python: `dataretrieval` (USGS) has `waterdata` functions ([docs](https://water.code-pages.usgs.gov/dataRetrieval/reference/read_waterdata_channel.html), R version; Python equivalent exists [bg]).

## Which sources attack the steep-reach high bias first (in order)
1. **1.1 + 1.2 gauges at 01169900 / 01171100** — discharge vs stage split (hydrology vs hydraulics). Cheapest, most diagnostic.
2. **1.5 crossing inverts/IWSE** — burnt-bed audit and digital-dam detection on the three reaches.
3. **1.3 channel measurements** — MA hydraulic geometry and measured n replacing Bieger 2015.
4. **1.6 FIS profiles** — reach-scale WSE profile at a known flow.
5. **2.1 + 2.2 rain gauges + RQI** — hill-town MRMS bias.
6. **1.9 lidar 1 m** — thalweg snapping and sub-grid tables.

For Dec 2023 over-wet roads: **3.1** (road-surface mask), **1.5** (culvert breaching removes artificial ponds behind road fill), **4.1 NFIP** and **4.4 Groundsource** (more negatives/positives), **2.6 SNODAS**.
